05 · Projects

Featured Project

A campus-wide infrastructure deployment, delivered end-to-end.

Role · Project Coordinator Scale · 4 Buildings / 13 Floors Scope · End-to-End Deployment

University Campus Network Infrastructure Project

Multi-building campus network deployment — planning, configuration, and commissioning.

1 Challenge

A multi-building campus (up to 13 floors each) needed unified, high-availability connectivity to support classrooms, labs, and administrative floors — with a structured design for segmentation, routing, and redundancy.

2 Approach & Implementation

  • Owned end-to-end planning, configuration, and deployment as Project Coordinator.
  • Directed design and rollout of 56 access-layer switches (14 per building) and 4 core switches, aggregated through a central MikroTik router.
  • Planned VLAN segmentation, IP addressing, and inter-building/inter-VLAN routing.
  • Configured the MikroTik router for inter-VLAN routing, NAT, and centralized internet gateway functions.
  • Coordinated port assignments, uplink/trunk connections, and redundancy planning.
  • Led on-site cabling, equipment placement, and testing/commissioning with technicians to minimize disruption.

3 Result

A stable, high-availability campus network spanning all 4 buildings — unified under centralized routing and internet distribution, with structured segmentation serving every floor and department.
